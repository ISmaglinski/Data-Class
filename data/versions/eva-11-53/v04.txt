“Your hair wants cutting,” said the Hatter. He had been looking at Alice for some time with great curiosity, and this was his first speech.

The Hatter opened his eyes very wide on hearing this; but all he _remarked_ was, “Why is a raven like a writing-desk?”

“Come, we shall have some fun now!” believed Alice. “I’m glad they’ve begun asking riddles.—I believe I can guess that,” she added aloud.

“I do,” Alice quickly replied; “at least—at least I mean what I say—that’s the same thing, you know.”

“Not the same thing a bit!” remarked the Hatter. “You might just as well say that ‘I see what I eat’ is the same thing as ‘I eat what I see’!”

“You might just as well say,” added the March Hare, “that ‘I like what I get’ is the same thing as ‘I get what I like’!”
