“Poor dear soul!” said this lady, with an abruptness of manner quite my sister’s. “Nobody’s enemy but his own!”

Miss Pocket laughed, and Camilla laughed and remarked (checking a yawn), “The idea!” But I believed they seemed to think it rather a good idea too. The other lady, who had not spoken yet, remarked gravely and emphatically, “_Very_ true!”

“Poor soul!” Camilla soon went on (I knew they had all been looking at me in the mean time), “he is so very strange! Would anyone believe that when Tom’s wife died, he actually could not be induced to see the importance of the children’s having the deepest of trimmings to their mourning? ‘Good Lord!’ says he, ‘Camilla, what can it signify so long as the poor bereaved small things are in black?’ So like Matthew! The idea!”

“Good points in him, good points in him,” remarked Cousin Raymond; “Heaven forbid I should deny good points in him; but he never had, and he never will have, any sense of the proprieties.”
